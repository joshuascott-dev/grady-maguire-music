The Story

Raised on red dirt and pine sap.

Grady Maguire writes the kind of country that comes from actually living it — out of Gilmer, Texas, deep in the Piney Woods, where the songs are about real people, real work, and the roads between.

He's taken his songs to stages across Texas, Oklahoma, and Arizona, building a following the old-fashioned way — one show at a time, one song at a time, with nothing to hide behind but the truth of it.

His first three singles arrive starting October 9, 2026 on Lazy Bend Records — recorded from originals written at home in East Texas.

Raw, honest, and nowhere to hide behind ’em.
